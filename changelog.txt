## [2026-09-27] MP harness round 2 — ball probes, knockback verification, reconnect dedup

- `bot.ts` new probes: `--balls` (30s listen → per-ball segment cadence, stamped-event age, spawn/die counters; measured ~1.8 segments/ball/s, event age p95 14ms — confirms the ball chop was the client-side 50ms render quantization, not the wire), `--watch` (join/left cadence + ghost-detection window).

- Balls smoothness: `balls.ts` render position now extrapolates `pos + vel*rem + ½g·rem²` over the un-stepped `netAcc` remainder — the sim only steps in 50ms quanta and the dropped remainder rendered as ~20fps chop.

## [2026-09-26] Sound engine — zero-asset procedural audio

- `core/audio.ts` (new): AudioEngine on raw Web Audio — master gain -> DynamicsCompressor -> destination, three buses (ui/sfx/world). No audio library: every sound is synthesized, zero downloads, latency-fine. Positional audio is cheap StereoPanner + distance gain + lowpass-with-distance (not HRTF — reads fine in a fast shooter, way cheaper). Voice cap 24 for one-shots; beyond 220m sounds don't spawn at all. `fps-volume` setting persists master volume.
- `core/sfx.ts` (new): the whole bank — per-weapon fire signatures (rocket square-chirp, chaingun burst, mortar thump, plasma/railgun zaps...), jump/land/dash/bounce/blink/slam/melee, hit markers (direct = brighter ping), hurt, nugget pickup, kill confirm, positional explosions + enemy telegraph cues (pounce, rakk dive, turretter launch, totem blink).
- Loops driven per-frame in `gameLoop.updateAudio`: jetpack roar (bandpass noise), ski scrape (highpass noise scaled by speed), wind rush (airspeed only — the cue that makes 100+u feel fast). Lazy-created, driven via setTargetAtTime, stopped when idle.
- Positional remote sounds: `sfx.shotAt` reuses each weapon's signature through the world bus, so remote players' shots pan/attenuate by distance. Enemy deaths (`enemyCue 'die'`) and explosions (`explosionAt`) are positional too.
- AudioContext unlocks on the pointer-lock click (the user gesture browsers require); everything no-ops before that.
- Settings panel: VOLUME slider next to MOUSE SENS; `fps-volume` default 0.7.
- Player.ts: new `onLand` hook + public `jetting`/`skiing` fields so the audio layer reads movement state without touching internals.
- NOTE: this commit sweeps in JP's in-flight WIP on the same files (config, weapons, protocol, species edits, new ambient/birds/tumbleweed/railgun modules) — entangled working tree, same as before.
