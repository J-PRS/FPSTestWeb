# KRIBES Session Notes — 2026-10-01

Reference doc for today's work: what shipped, what the sims taught us, and
the open idea/plan backlog. Standing context: KRIBES = browser multiplayer
FPS, "Tribes gameplay, Krunker performance" — Three.js client, Bun server,

## Design philosophy (binding)

- **Guidelines, not laws.** Nothing bot-side is 100% — strong suggestions
  scaled by personality/duty. Bots adapt to environment dynamically, no
  pre-baked A* routes.
- **Resources are real.** Belt items, deploys, skills, and self-propulsion
  all spend something — health, charges, or jet energy. Spam is a
  resource decision, not free utility.
- **Shared model.** Weapon stats, roles, goals, loadout kits live in
  shared/ so wire bots, offline bots, and humans see identical rules.
- **Debugger first.** Investigate before fixing; don't modify tests to
  pass unless the change is legitimately out of scope (e.g. funding belt
  charges in deploy-cap tests).

## Behavioral debugging methodology (what worked — keep doing this)

The orbit/stall hunt was solved by instrumenting before theorizing. Techniques
that produced evidence instead of guesses:

1. **Decision tracing at stage boundaries** — `FPS_TRACE=<id-substring>` makes
   think() log `pos / goal / d0 (pickDest) / d1 (lines.plan) / d2 (steerer)` per
   think. Watching a single bot's chain end-to-end located the guilty stage in
   one run, where aggregate metrics couldn't. This is the industry pattern
   (Unreal AI Debugging, utility-AI decision snapshots): trace decisions, not
   outcomes.
2. **KPI probes that discriminate hypotheses** — each soak KPI was added to
   *separate* candidate causes: `flips` (does the goal teleport?), `prog`
   (do windows gain distance?), `align` (does velocity point at the goal?),
   `hunt`/`inSight`/`foeM` (do enemies ever meet?). One soak run then kills a
   whole family of theories.
3. **Why-codes on every gate** — `jetWhy` existed and made the diveRun bug
   readable: jets were off not from missing intent but from a veto reading a
   flattened value. Any gate that can silence a behavior should expose a named
   reason.
4. **First-principles definitions, not proxies** — "stuck" was originally
   measured as low displacement; orbiting defeats that proxy. Redefining it as
   *no progress toward goal* caught the real failure. Same pattern: "downhill
   run" measured off the step's flattened y instead of the route's true
   altitude.
5. **Stage isolation tests** — botMoves/botEscape unit tests pin each stage's
   contract; when the soak misbehaves, the failing stage is already boxed.

### The orbit cascade (root causes, in order found)
- **Goal churn**: entry probe re-scored every think with a flat +12 margin →
  goals teleported 100-300m/s (`flips=238`). Fixed by distance-scaled decisive
  gap `min(120, 25 + dStand*0.2)` — rescoring stays live per JP's directive,
  switching just costs conviction (`flips→0.3`).
- **Side-valley rolls**: BotLines scored downhill drops over goal progress →
  perpetual perpendicular valley runs (`align=0.00`). Fixed by progress floor
  `gained < d*0.4` with an energy escape hatch (eScore>12) — JP: a detour that
  banks speed is legitimate.
- **Memory-trail tangent orbits**: slope danger masks all goal-ward slots at a
  plateau rim → "least dangerous" wins → tangents; trail crumbs repel the last
  position → self-sustaining 8m circles. Addressed by the goal-distance stuck
  window.
- **The jets that never fire (biggest find)**: `diveRun` in botMoves.hints
  read `dest.y`/`gd` — but every stage flattens the step's y to pos.y and the
  step is ~8m → diveRun was unconditionally true → `jetHold=0` every tick →
  jets ONLY ever fired from the 1.1s escape burst. A 50m plateau rim was
  unclimbable. Fixed by reading altitude off `ctx.objective` (the route), not
  the flattened step. Result: kills 22→80, press 105→55/44m, e_med +36,
  inSight 1%→13% in one change.

### The wave arc (why pushes produced no support — found via wave probe)
The ring autopsy showed pushes dying at ~41-51m with `sup@0` — zero allies
near the stand. Probing tasked members directly (`debugTasks` + wave
sample line) traced it through three stacked causes:

- **Task re-roll**: `cmd.update` cleared all assignments every 2s and
  re-picked "nearest volunteer" — the job kept swapping to whoever just
  respawned at home, so no assignee ever completed a leg. Fix: tasks
  persist between passes; a task dies only with its job slot or its
  assignee, understaffed slots top up instead of re-rolling (4021043).
- **Call window vs transit**: the 10s/8s call tasked members who needed
  ~15-20s to cross midfield (~12m/s effective through fights). Posting
  at capper-ETA<30s lets the same slow flood actually land — first
  multi-cap soaks followed (37d54d8).
- **Ring-entry reroute**: tasked members inside the DMZ were re-routed
  to ring entries like untasked pressers; the committed-final-run
  shortcut was capper-only (4021043).

Remaining: `sup@0` at push resolution — wave members arrive but not
screening the grab itself (median member dist ~170m, near ~4%). Transit
speed through midfield fights (~12m/s) is the limiting term; entry
speed ~16-18m/s needs 30+ to blow through interceptors. Drop-bonus
scoring helps picks choose run-in descents (cfe341d).

Foe/free probe split: wave transit is ~85% uncontested (free ~14.5k vs
foe ~2.5k samples) — the slow transit is locomotion, not interception.
Dry-tank veto on heel blasts removed (energyOf<10 gated the fuel-free
launch it was argued for) — da31214. Katabatic soak: the moat stalls
the wave identically (median ~290m, near0%, free 11k/foe 53) — the wall
is a systemic locomotion blocker for every crosser, not a capper-only
pathology. Awaiting JP's pick on the moat mechanic (climb budget /
momentum-carry / accept-as-T2).
