# KRIBES Session Notes — 2026-10-01

Reference doc for today's work: what shipped, what the sims taught us, and
the open idea/plan backlog. Standing context: KRIBES = browser multiplayer
FPS, "Tribes gameplay, Krunker performance" — Three.js client, Bun server,
shared deterministic sims.

## Design philosophy (binding)

- **Guidelines, not laws.** Nothing bot-side is 100% — strong suggestions
  scaled by personality/duty. Bots adapt to environment dynamically, no
  pre-baked A* routes.
- **Resources are real.** Belt items, deploys, skills, and self-propulsion
  all spend something — health, charges, or jet energy. Spam is a
  resource decision, not free utility.
- **Shared model.** Weapon stats, roles, goals, loadout kits live in
  shared/ so wire bots, offline bots, and humans see identical rules.
- **Debugger first.** Investigate before fixing; don't modify tests to
  pass unless the change is legitimately out of scope (e.g. funding belt
  charges in deploy-cap tests).

## Shipped today (4 commits)

### 1. Healer archetype
- **HealBeam** weapon: energy-fed latch beam on allies (gentle latch —
  less sticky than Mercy, drops when ally exits range/cone). Energy-as-ammo:
  pocket healing costs the healer's own jet mobility.
- **HealGrenade** belt: strong burst heal, allies only.
- **`medic` role** (squad 7+, forces full heal kit) + hybrid heal-carrying
  loadout rows below the threshold — partial healers exist without the job.
- `medicGoal`: pocket the carrier, shadow the stand press ~40m back.
- `healAlly` keyed off carried kit, not role tag. Technician keeps repair
  beam (mechanicals) — healer is the organic counterpart.

### 2. Belt-charge economy + instant-skill energy
- `beltCharges`: **5 on spawn, −1 per belt throw, +1 per nugget pickup
  (cap 5), −2 per deployable**. Server truth for wire humans (spawn +
  deploy claims) and bots (lobAt + same deploy path).
- `BELT_THROW_WEAPONS` gates exclusive belt types; Sticky excluded
  (weaponType shared with the sticky launcher).
- **Instant skills ante 50 energy**: blink (post wall-clear ray),
  dash/slam at activation, warp at teleport (not throw), bounce at the
  landing fire. Fizzles stay free. `skillUse` claim mirrors the ante on
  the server's move shadow.
- Deploy-cap tests funded to 99 charges (they test cap-recycling, not economy).

### 3. Bot resource mindfulness + disc-jump family + pursuit + concussion
- **`spendOk` value tiers**: high-value spends (propel, heals, deploys,
  saboteur lobs) pay if affordable; casual lobs/armor nades keep a
  2-charge reserve — dropped to 1 when a nugget is in reach, 0 when the
  pocket is already full (the +1 would waste anyway).
- **Disc-jump launch family**:
  - *Heel blast* (grounded): slope-aware — back-and-flat speed-disc on
    steep descents, under-body pop on flat/uphill.
  - *Pull-disc surf*: implosion lobbed ~18m ahead for pull-belt carriers
    (~80% beltPull draw / ~40% otherwise).
  - *Crest blast* (airborne): straight-down shot when the hill drops away
    under a fast climber — "lose contact, shoot the floor" height trick.
  - Long pursuits (60m+) may boost **mid-volley** — out-shooting never
    closes ground on a fleeing carrier.
- **ldefense chaser kit**: mortar/rocket far → railgun, shotgun/flak near
  → lightning gun (beam tracking beats projectile lead vs jinking carriers).
- **Concussion strip**: ImpactGrenade blast on an enemy carrier →
  `dropFlagCarrier` → flag fumbles (T2 impact nade).
- **fastSoak telemetry**: per-bot `avgE`/`peakE` (specific energy
  ½v²+g·hAGL — un-gameable by llamas), `e_med` KPI field, heel/pull/crest
  launch counters per bot + top-3.

### 4. Self-splash cost + chaser trials
- **`SELF_SPLASH_MAX = 30`**: bots' own blasts deal up to ~30 self-damage
  inside pad distance (was zero — free launches); farther out, normal
  scaled splash. Humans pay the same client-side (was ~10 at feet scale).
- **Desperation guideline**: under 45hp bots hold fire — *except* a
  chaser whose carrier is far (gap>60m) or pulling away along the chase
  line (closing rate negative) may still launch; never below 35hp where
  the blast would outright kill it.
- Launch spam dropped ~6× on comparable soaks (4563 → 766 heels).
- **chaseTrial scenario** (`tests/scenarios/chaseTrial.test.ts`): 1v1
  capper-escape weapon trials — chaser forced to one weapon per trial,
  measures shots/hits/gap/killT/capT.
