- 2026-10-15 - a custom field must MATCH THE T2 RELIEF PROFILE, not just "have hills". Two hard-won specifics: (a) NORMALIZE the field to the full byte range - `height` in every palette is byte/255 (hscale = 125*heightScale cancels the world scale), so a field that only fills 0..0.56 leaves every palette stuck in its low band; (b) features must be ADDITIVE and domain-warped - `mix(h, const, t)` turns a "mesa" into a PIT whenever the constant sits below the local fBm mean, and unwarped radial/circular ops stamp literal circles (see hillshade.js).
- 2026-10-15 - do NOT author `fog.horizon` on a custom map (the same mistake I made on the first pass). syncFog copies the sun-tinted `fog.color` into BOTH scene.fog.color and the terrain's fog uniform, and then sets the sky's horizon band to `fogHorizon() ?? tinted` - i.e. with no override the sky's horizon IS the colour the ground melts into, so fully-fogged terrain dissolves seamlessly into the sky (this is what makes the default sand map read so cleanly). An explicit horizon puts a differently-coloured band exactly where terrain meets sky. Mood belongs in `fog.color` + `atmosphere`, not a horizon override.
